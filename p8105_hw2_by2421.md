I imported the three sheets, standardized column names, and removed rows
without dumpster numbers. I rounded the sports ball counts to integers
and made the year variable consistent across datasets. I treated the
nonnumeric plastic bottle entry “dive” as missing. I added a trash wheel
identifier before combining the datasets.

The dataset now contains 1269 observations and 15 variables. Each row is
for a dumpster collection record. Key variables include trash wheel,
dumpster number, date, weight in tons, volume in cubic yards, and counts
of individual types of trash. Professor Trash Wheel collected 294.96
tons of trash. Gwynnda collected 18120 cigarette butts in June 2022.

I skipped the descriptive first row, standardized column names, and
treated periods as missing values. Sex and APOE4 status were converted
to factors. I excluded participants whose MCI onset age was less than or
equal to their baseline age. Participants with missing onset age were
kept because they remained MCI free during follow-up. The original
dataset contains 483 participants. Four already had MCI at baseline,
leaving 479 eligible participants. Among these, 93 developed MCI. The
average baseline age was 65.03 years. Among women, 30% were APOE4
carriers.
